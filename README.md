# Engineering-portfolio
This repository contains a selection of engineering projects focused on mechanical design, numerical simulation, manufacturing processes and engineering automation. 

The objective of this portfolio is to demonstrate practical engineering skills through a combination of CAD modelling, finite element analysis, numerical methods and Python-based engineering tools.

Python projects developed to automate engineering calculations, explore design spaces and optimize technical parameters.

Projects include:

- Manufacturing process parameter optimization

- Mechanical design automation

- Numerical optimization

- Engineering data analysis

## CAD & Mechanical Design

Mechanical design projects developed using FreeCad and Autodesk Fusion.

Projects include:

- Mechanical assemblies

- Reverse engineering

- Gearbox design

- Manufacturing drawings

- Component modelling

## FEA & Meshing

Structural simulation projects focused on:

- Finite element modelling

- Mesh generation and refinement

- Boundary condition definition

- Stress and deformation analysis

- Result interpretation

- Mesh convergence studies

## Kinematics

Projects involving mechanical systems, motion analysis and trajectory generation.

Topics may include:

- Kinematic analysis

- Robot trajectory generation

- Mechanical simulation

- Motion planning

## AI & Experimental Projects

Projects involving LLMs, local agents and automatization.
